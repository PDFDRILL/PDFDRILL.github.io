The dark shaded circular tensors in step (b) are "effective features" formed from \(m\) different linear combinations of many original features.
7DiagramFig 7: Update (a) of bond tensor \(B^{\ell}\), (b) restoration of MPS form, and (c) advancing a projected training input before optimizing the tensors at the next bond.
